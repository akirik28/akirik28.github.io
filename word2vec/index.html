§ 02 · The problemword2vec · Mikolov et al. 2013

What’s the problem with traditional NLP?

  • Words were one-hot vectors: just a position, no meaning.
  • This made NLP systems rigid, hard to scale and inefficient.
  • They capture no information about word similarity.
One-hot encodingWord embedding

[0.21, 0.97, −0.33, …]
“dog”=
[0.24, 0.93, −0.31, …]

No semantic relationship

Every pair of words is equally far apart.

Close relationship

Similar meaning → similar numbers → nearby points.

2007, 2009

word2vec

§ 09 · Continuous Bag-of-Wordsword2vec · Mikolov et al. 2013

Continuous Bag-of-Words context → center word

  1. 1

    N context words as input, target word w(t)

  2. 2

    Embed each word: N vectors of dimension D

  3. 3

    Average them: x̄ = 1N Σ ei

  4. 4

    Predict the center word with a hierarchical softmax

  5. 5

    Compute the loss

  6. 6

    Repeat

Cost per training step

Q = N×Daverage
embedding + D×log2Vefficient
softmax

V = 100,000vocabulary size
log2V ≈ 17scores with a hierarchical softmax

§ 10 · Skip-gramword2vec · Mikolov et al. 2013

Skip-gram center word → context

  1. Input: the center word

  2. Vector embedding x = ewas ∈ ℝD

  3. Predict the context words with a hierarchical softmax, as in CBOW

  4. Cross-entropy loss: Loss = −log P(correct word | center word)

  5. Repeat

The smaller the probability, the larger the loss.
