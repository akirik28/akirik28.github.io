**Uygulamanın formül puanı:** ≈23/30 (kanıt 10 · ikna 7: soru 0/3 · dil 6: 4 kısaltma).

## En çok puan getirecek 6 düzeltme
1. **Soru işareti koy:** üç jüri sorusunun sonuna "?" — 3 puan.
2. **Kısaltma yok:** it's → it is, isn't → is not, I'm → I am, thats → that is.
3. **Büyük harf:** Elizabeth Proctor, John Proctor, Mary Warren (iki r değil, tek r: *Mary*), Betty, Abigail, Salem.
4. **Fiil hataları:** "wants her to be hang" → *wants her to hang* · "she only tell lie once" → *she told only one lie* · "Elizabeth is not tell lie" → *Elizabeth never lied* · "think logical" → *think logically* · "get his place" → *take her place*.
5. **Riskli iki cümleyi değiştir:** "middle-aged" sil; "the baby will die too" yerine *"a child will be born in a prison cell and grow up without a mother."*
6. **Üçlemede "ambush"** anlamsız kalıyor: *"truth over jealousy"* daha iyi.

## Düzeltilmiş hâli (senin cümlelerin, hataları giderilmiş — ezberlemek için)

Ladies and gentlemen of the jury, my client, Elizabeth Proctor, stands accused of witchcraft and of trying to kill Abigail Williams. I will not pretend that Elizabeth Proctor is perfect; she lied in court about her husband's affair. But that lie is not a crime, and it is not witchcraft. Elizabeth Proctor is not guilty, because (1) Abigail wants her to hang so that she can have John Proctor for herself, (2) the poppet belongs to Mary Warren, and (3) she told only one lie, and she told it to protect her husband.

First, Abigail accused my client because she wants John Proctor for herself. The record shows that, long before any poppet, Betty reveals that Abigail drank a charm to kill Elizabeth (Miller 18). This proves that Abigail's real reason to accuse my client is to take her place as John Proctor's wife. Furthermore, Elizabeth had dismissed Abigail from her house, which gave Abigail every reason to hate her (Miller 105). Ask yourselves: what would you do if a girl wanted your place, and that was the only reason you were on trial?

Moreover, the poppet that the prosecution calls a tool of witchcraft belongs to Mary Warren. The play shows that Mary Warren openly says that she made the poppet in court and gave it to Elizabeth (Miller 71). This shows that the poppet was never my client's, so she cannot be accused because of it. In addition, Mary says that Abigail was sitting beside her when she made the poppet (Miller 72). Is it fair to blame Elizabeth for a poppet that is not even hers?

Finally, Elizabeth told only one lie in her life, and she told it to protect her husband. The text shows that her only lie was told out of love, to protect her husband's name (Miller 105). This means that Elizabeth cares for John more than for herself, even in a courtroom. Most importantly, in the last act Elizabeth honestly admits her own coldness (Miller 127). What would you have done if your life were on the line and you were asked to give up the person you love?

Picture Elizabeth Proctor tonight. She is a pregnant mother sitting in a cold prison cell. If you convict her, a child will be born in a prison cell and grow up without a mother.

Ultimately, the prosecution asks you to choose rumor over evidence, jealousy over truth, and lies over love. Elizabeth Proctor made mistakes, but she is no witch and no criminal. I ask you to think logically. Find Elizabeth Proctor not guilty. Send her home.
